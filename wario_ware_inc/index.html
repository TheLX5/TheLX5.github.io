Goal

Obtain enough Flower items in the session to enable the credits level which will trigger the goal.

You current flower count is displayed on the Bizhawk Client.

Locations

Most games are supported as potential locations in the game, you can toggle almost every single one.

Games Locations Available
Character Stages

Clear the stage.

Obtain certain high scores. Those are optional except the lowest one (tied to the clear condition).
Microgames

Obtain at least 5 points.

Obtain enough points to get a flower rank.

Players can also decide how many of these will be on a given session.
Extra stages

Obtain certain point thresholds.

They're low enough to not feel quite grindy.

Items

There's not that much variety of items in this implementation other than the Flowers, Stage unlocks and Microgame unlocks.

Players can decide whether microgames are unlocked via "Microgame Bundles" or standalone microgame items.
