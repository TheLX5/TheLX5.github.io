3.1.1

  • Implemented DamageLink (named SharedDamage for tagging purposes)
    • Whenever players take a hit they send a Damage signal to others
    • Can be toggled in the Manager
    • Currently DKC2 handles it like this:
      • Other DKC2 players get hurt when the cumulative damage gets to 80
      • Sends a 80 damage signal when the player gets hurt in any way, including extra hits
  • Apparently the *Link options in the manager did absolutely nothing, that's now fixed
  • Replaced bundled docs' text with links to my WIP webpage.

3.0.0

    • There's a small icon in the bottom right corner. If Diddy the reward will be extra lives, if Dixie the reward will be extra Backup DK Barrels
  • There's a way to visualize in-game how many Backup DK Barrel items are left. A counter will appear on top of the player when they:
    • Enter a level
    • Use a Backup DK Barrel
    • Gain a Backup DK Barrel
    • Get hurt

  • A Diddy icon is shown instead of a Banana when you have an extra hit active

  • Renamed player_palettes and player_palette_filters options to palettes and palette_filters respectively to properly match their new purpose
  • Altered the default options to have a better "new player" experience

    • Swanky @ Galleon has all of his challenges available for free
  • You can no longer visit Cranky with L + R during the map
  • Reduced the chance of generating Banana Coins as filler items
