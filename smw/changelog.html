1.3.5

  • Implemented DamageLink (named SharedDamage for tagging purposes)
    • Whenever players take a hit they send a Damage signal to others
    • Can be toggled in the Manager
    • Currently Waffles handles it like this:
      • Other Waffles players get hurt when the cumulative damage gets to 80
      • Sends a 80 damage signal when the player loses a powerup, loses a heart or dies
      • Sends a 20 damage signal when the player loses Yoshi
  • Removed RingLink handling from the code (didn't work on Waffles)
  • Made Universal Tracker aware of the Decoupled options
