Goal

Obtain enough Medal items in the session to enable the credits level which will trigger the goal.

You current medal count is displayed on the game select menu.

Locations

Clearing levels with different rankings are considered locations as expected.

  • OK Clears
  • Superb Clears
  • Perfect Clears

There's another set of locations named Clear which is basically an OK Clear on the background.

To make things flow better, Perfect Campaigns will start appearing as soon as possible and they only take "1 level" of delay instead of RNG based on the medal amount you have at the moment.

Items

There's not that much variety of items in this implementation other than the Medals and level unlocks.

Players can decide whether levels are unlocked via "Remix Bundles" or standalone stage items.
